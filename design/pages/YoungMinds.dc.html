In 12 weeks, we walk you through these modules.

  1. Weeks 1–2Communicate with Clarity

    Organise your thoughts into clear, accurate sentences.

    Active listening, sentence formation, pronunciation, stress, rhythm and intonation.

  2. Weeks 3–4Speak with Confidence

    Start conversations and express thoughts naturally.

    Introducing yourself, fluency, asking questions, describing experiences, voice modulation.

  3. Weeks 5–6Conversations Beyond the Script

    Speak comfortably, even when talk takes an unexpected turn.

    Everyday topics, opinions, storytelling, group discussions.

  4. Weeks 7–8Handle, Influence & Solve

    Think on your feet and respond without hesitation.

    Difficult conversations, disagreements, persuasion, problem-solving.

  5. Weeks 9–10Communication in Action

    Use English in real work situations, professionally.

    Understanding needs, explaining simply, handling queries, customer-facing situations.

  6. Weeks 11–12Work Smarter

    Be ready for interviews and the workplace.

    Interview basics, email and workplace writing, managing emotions.

How it works.

  1. In classLearn

    Live on Zoom, three times a week, 1.5 hours each.

  2. Outside classPractise on your own and with your peers

    Activities on our platform keep the habit going during the week.

  3. In classThe 70:30 rule

    70% of every class is speaking: role-plays, discussions and real work situations. 30% is grammar, built into the speaking.

  4. Outside classGet feedback

    Peers and facilitators respond, and the learning app tracks your progress.
